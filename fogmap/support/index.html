基本介紹

整張地圖一開始都蓋著迷霧，你走過的地方才會被點亮。App 會自動記錄足跡，統計你制霸了哪些鄉鎮市區、縣市、國家與名勝。你的位置與足跡只存在你的 iPhone 上。

開始使用

  • 允許位置：第一次開啟時，先選「使用 App 期間」，之後在「設定」＞「迷霧地圖」＞「位置」改成「永遠」，App 才能在背景持續記錄。
  • 旅行模式：左下角的「旅行模式」會記錄得更頻繁，適合長途旅行，比較耗電。
  • 從相簿匯入：按相簿圖示，用照片裡的拍攝位置，一次點亮你以前去過的地方。
  • 打卡：按「打卡」，為現在的地點加上名稱、心得和照片。
  • 制霸：點上方卡片的「制霸」，看臺灣各縣市與鄉鎮、世界各國及其他地區的進度。
  • 移動軌跡：右下角的路線圖示，查看每一天的路線。
  • 照片地圖：照片依拍攝地點顯示在地圖上，放大會逐步展開；也可以用右側按鈕關閉。

進階版

臺灣的迷霧地圖、背景記錄、臺灣制霸和每日軌跡永遠免費。進階版（月訂閱、年訂閱或買斷）另外解鎖：世界與各地區制霸、世界名勝與維基百科簡介、臺灣以外的慶祝卡與通知、年度足跡分享圖、軌跡匯出（GPX）。

  • 恢復購買：換手機或重新安裝後，打開進階版畫面，按「恢復購買」。
  • 管理或取消訂閱：iPhone 的「設定」＞你的名字＞「訂閱項目」。訂閱會在期間結束前自動續訂，到期前至少 24 小時取消就不會再扣款。
  • 退款：購買與退款由 Apple 處理，請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請。

常見問題

  • 地圖沒有被點亮，或背景沒有記錄？確認位置權限是「永遠」、「精確位置」已開啟，並且沒有開啟「低耗電模式」。iOS 在低電量時會暫停背景定位。
  • 名勝簡介一直轉圈或顯示讀不到？簡介需要網路，由維基百科提供。沒有網路或維基百科暫時限制連線時，可以按「在維基百科開啟」直接用瀏覽器看。
  • 為什麼我走過的名勝沒有算進去？需要在名勝附近（數百公尺內）點亮過迷霧才算。
  • 可以把資料搬到新手機嗎？資料只存在手機上。用 iPhone 的備份或「快速開始」換機，足跡會一併搬過去。
  • 如何刪除資料？在 App 內刪除單日軌跡與打卡紀錄，或直接刪除 App，會移除 App 儲存的所有資料。

About

The whole map starts under fog and only the places you have been light up. The app records your footprints automatically and counts the townships, counties, countries and landmarks you have conquered. Your location and footprints are stored only on your iPhone.

Getting started

  • Allow location: choose "While Using the App" the first time, then set Settings > FogMap > Location to "Always" so the app can keep recording in the background.
  • Travel Mode: the button at the lower left records more often. It suits long trips and uses more battery.
  • Import from Photos: tap the photo icon to light up places you have already been, using the locations in your photos.
  • Check in: tap "Check In" to add a name, notes and photos to where you are.
  • Conquest: tap "Conquest" on the top card to see your progress across Taiwan's counties and townships, the world's countries and other regions.
  • Tracks: the route icon at the lower right shows each day's route.
  • Photo map: photos appear on the map where they were taken and unfold as you zoom in; you can switch them off with the button on the right.

Premium

  • Refunds: purchases and refunds are handled by Apple; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

FAQ

  • The map isn't lighting up, or nothing records in the background? Make sure location access is "Always" with Precise Location on, and that Low Power Mode is off. iOS pauses background location when the battery is low.
  • A landmark summary keeps spinning or can't be loaded? Summaries need a connection and come from Wikipedia. If you're offline or Wikipedia is temporarily limiting requests, tap "Open in Wikipedia" to read it in the browser.
  • Why doesn't a landmark I passed count? You need to have lit fog near the landmark (within a few hundred metres).
  • Can I move my data to a new phone? The data lives only on the phone. Switching with an iPhone backup or Quick Start brings your footprints along.
  • How do I delete my data? Delete a day's track and your check-ins inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stores.
