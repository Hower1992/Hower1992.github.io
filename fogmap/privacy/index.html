迷霧地圖 FogMap
隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日　適用版本：1.0.0 以後

繁體中文 English 使用說明與支援

重點摘要

  • 你的位置、足跡、軌跡、打卡和照片資訊，只存在你的 iPhone 上，開發者收不到。你主動傳給朋友的打卡，只會交給你選的人。
  • App 沒有帳號系統，也沒有開發者自己的伺服器，沒有廣告，沒有使用者分析或追蹤工具。
  • 只有在你查看名勝簡介時，App 會向維基百科請求該條目的文字與縮圖，不會附上你的位置。
  • 地圖由 Apple 地圖提供。本 App 免費，沒有內購，開發者不會取得任何付款資料。

1. 位置資料

迷霧地圖（以下稱「本 App」）使用你的位置，把你走過的地方從迷霧中點亮。你同意「永遠」使用位置後，本 App 會在背景以省電方式持續記錄；若只允許「使用 App 期間」，則只在 App 開啟時記錄。你也可以選擇「旅行模式」，記錄得更頻繁、更完整。

本 App 會儲存兩種資料：已點亮的地圖格子（含第一次點亮的日期），以及每天的移動軌跡（位置點與時間）。它們只存在你的裝置上，不會傳送給開發者或任何第三方。

2. 照片

如果你授權存取照片，本 App 會讀取照片的拍攝位置與日期，用來一次點亮你以前去過的地方，並在地圖上依拍攝地點顯示照片縮圖。縮圖由 Apple「照片」在你的裝置上產生。照片內容、拍攝位置與縮圖不會上傳。

你可以隨時在 iPhone 的「設定」＞「隱私權與安全性」＞「照片」中改成「選取的照片」或關閉權限，不影響其他功能。

3. 打卡紀錄

你建立的打卡（名稱、心得、位置、時間，以及你選擇附上的照片）只存在你的裝置上。開發者看不到這些資料。

4. 存在你裝置上的其他資料

本 App 會在裝置上儲存偏好設定、已看過的解鎖慶祝紀錄，以及名勝簡介的快取（保留最近查看過的內容，方便離線時使用）。這些資料可能依你的 Apple 裝置設定，被納入 iCloud 或電腦的系統備份。

5. 通知

如果你允許，本 App 會在你走到新地方時，在你的 iPhone 上顯示本機通知。通知完全在裝置上產生，沒有任何推播伺服器參與。

6. 會連上網路的功能

  • 地圖：地圖畫面使用 Apple 地圖（MapKit）。Apple 依其 隱私權政策處理地圖相關請求。
  • 名勝簡介：你打開一個名勝時，本 App 會向維基百科（Wikimedia 基金會）請求該條目的標題對應的摘要與縮圖。請求只包含條目標題與你的語言偏好；維基媒體伺服器會看到你的 IP 位址，並依其 隱私權政策處理。本 App 不會告訴維基百科你的位置，也不會告訴它你是否真的去過那個名勝。

除了上述項目，本 App 沒有整合任何廣告、使用者分析、當機回報或追蹤服務。

7. 匯出與分享

你可以把軌跡匯出成 GPX 檔案、把年度足跡圖分享出去，或把某一個打卡傳給朋友。這些動作都由你主動操作，內容會交給你選擇的 App 或對象（例如 AirDrop、LINE、訊息），之後由對方和該服務的隱私權政策管轄。

傳給朋友的打卡包含：地點名稱、心得、位置、日期，以及你選擇附上的照片和你填的名字。不會包含你的足跡、軌跡或即時位置。內容只存在你傳出的檔案或連結裡，不經過開發者的伺服器，開發者看不到。

收到朋友打卡時，本 App 會先顯示內容並詢問是否加入。加入後只存在你的手機上，你可以隨時隱藏或刪除，不會通知對方。

8. 兒童隱私

本 App 不是專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，開發者也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

9. 你的選擇與資料刪除

  • 你可以隨時在 iPhone 的「設定」＞「隱私權與安全性」＞「定位服務」中，改變或關閉本 App 的位置權限。
  • 你可以在 App 內刪除單日的軌跡與打卡紀錄。
  • 刪除本 App 會移除它在裝置上儲存的所有資料，包含足跡與軌跡。系統備份由你在 Apple 設定中管理。
  • 因為資料不在開發者手上，開發者無法替你查詢、匯出或刪除這些資料；你可以直接在 App 內操作。

10. 聯絡我們

如果你主動寄信給我們，我們會收到你的 Email 地址及信件內容，只用於回覆你的問題。請不要附上不必要的私人資料。

FogMap Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0.0 and later

Summary

  • Your location, footprints, tracks, check-ins and photo information stay on your iPhone. The developer never receives them. A check-in you send to a friend goes only to the person you pick.
  • The app has no account system, no developer-operated server, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Only when you open a landmark does the app ask Wikipedia for that article's text and thumbnail. It does not send your location.
  • Maps are provided by Apple Maps. The app is free with no in-app purchases, so the developer never receives any payment details.

1. Location data

FogMap ("the app") uses your location to light up the places you have been. If you allow "Always" access, the app records in the background in a low-power way; with "While Using the App" it records only while the app is open. You can also turn on Travel Mode, which records more often and more completely.

The app stores two kinds of data: the map cells you have lit (with the date each was first lit) and your daily movement tracks (location points and times). They stay on your device and are not sent to the developer or any third party.

2. Photos

If you grant photo access, the app reads each photo's capture location and date to light up places you have already been, and shows photo thumbnails on the map where they were taken. Thumbnails are produced by Apple Photos on your device. Photo content, locations and thumbnails are never uploaded. You can switch to "Selected Photos" or turn access off in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos at any time without affecting other features.

3. Check-ins

Check-ins you create (name, notes, location, time and any photo you choose to attach) are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see them.

4. Other data on your device

The app stores preferences, a record of which unlock celebrations you have seen, and a cache of landmark summaries (the most recently viewed, for offline use). This data may be included in iCloud or computer backups according to your Apple device settings.

5. Notifications

If you allow them, the app shows local notifications on your iPhone when you reach a new place. They are generated entirely on the device; no push server is involved.

6. Features that use the network

  • Maps: The map screens use Apple Maps (MapKit). Apple handles map requests under its Privacy Policy.
  • Landmark summaries: When you open a landmark, the app asks Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) for the summary and thumbnail of that article. The request contains only the article title and your language preference; Wikimedia servers will see your IP address and handle it under their Privacy Policy. The app does not tell Wikipedia your location or whether you have actually visited the landmark.

Apart from the above, the app integrates no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.

7. Export and sharing

You can export tracks as a GPX file, share your year-in-review picture, or send a single check-in to a friend. These actions are started by you, and the content goes to the app or person you choose (for example AirDrop, LINE or Messages), whose own privacy policy then applies.

A check-in sent to a friend contains the place name, notes, location and date, plus the photo and name you choose to include. It never contains your footprints, tracks or live location. The content lives only in the file or link you send; it does not pass through any developer server and the developer cannot see it.

When you receive a friend's check-in, the app shows it first and asks whether to add it. Once added it is stored only on your phone, and you can hide or delete it at any time without notifying the sender.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not designed for children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices and deleting data

  • You can change or turn off the app's location access at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services.
  • You can delete a day's track and your check-ins inside the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all data it stores on your device, including footprints and tracks. System backups are managed by you in Apple settings.
  • Because the data is not held by the developer, the developer cannot look up, export or delete it for you; you can do so in the app.
